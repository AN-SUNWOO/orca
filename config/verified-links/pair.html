Pair Orca Mobile

This link pairs your phone with Orca on your computer. It opens in the Orca app when Orca Mobile is installed and up to date.

Seeing this page instead?

  1. Update Orca Mobile, then open the link again.
  2. Or open Orca Mobile, tap Pair, and scan the QR code shown in Orca on your computer under Settings → Mobile.
  3. Or copy the pairing code and paste it in Orca Mobile under Pair → Paste code.

Don't have the app? Download Orca Mobile.

Treat the pairing code like a password. It is in the part of this link after “#”, which your browser keeps on this device and never sends to onorca.dev.
